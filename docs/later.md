# Later

Ideas deliberately parked so they stay out of the current milestone.

- Home management (chores, groceries, bills)
- Sensors (presence, light, air quality, tank monitor)
- Sleep sounds
- Routines that run at a set time
- Editing calendar events from the Hub
- Habits with weekly goals
- More Journey Modes (car, bus, boat or ship, kayak)
- Fantasy art style for Journeys
- A demo mode with sample data, for showing the Hub on the portfolio
- Settings screen, opened from a button at the top right of Today next to the time. The spot is reserved in the design; the button is left out until the screen exists. First settings: Fahrenheit or Celsius, day rollover time, fallback weather location, and whether colors follow the sun or stay on one palette
- Moon extras on the dial, such as a blood moon during a total lunar eclipse

## If the Hub is ever released

- Weather: Open-Meteo's free tier covers non-commercial use only. A release needs their paid plan or a different service.
- Place names: the weather's town name comes from OpenStreetMap's free lookup, which is for light use only. A release needs a paid lookup or its own.
- Spotify: personal-project access is capped at five users. A release needs Spotify's approval for wider access.
- Sign-in: add proper accounts and a setup flow.
